What Is Man? (1906)

A census of known copies of Mark Twain’s anonymous first edition

In July 1906 the De Vinne Press of New York printed What Is Man? anonymously in an edition of 250 numbered copies (BAL 3490), copyrighted by J. W. Bothwell. This census records every copy whose number can be documented, from library catalogs, limitation-leaf photographs supplied by libraries, dealer listings, and auction records.

As of 11 October 2026 the census records 70 known numbered copies of the 250. Copies held by private individuals are listed as “Private collection” without further detail.
